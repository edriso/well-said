# Six-month curriculum

Well Said contains 184 daily workouts. That number is deliberate: a six-calendar-month span can contain as many as 184 dates, so a 183-lesson catalog is not sufficient for every possible start month.

## Coverage

The rotation balances ten situation families: Everyday, Work, Social, Travel, Food, Home, Services, Community, Wellbeing, and Ideas. Each extended category contributes 18 lessons. The four foundational workouts add a little extra weight to Work, Food, and Social practice.

Difficulty rotates across A2–B1, B1–B2, and B2–C1, with B1–B2 intentionally receiving the largest share. Speaking focuses cycle through content-word stress, thought groups, linking, question intonation, polite rhythm, contrastive stress, turn-taking, clear details, expressive delivery, and calm clarification.

## Scheduling guarantee

The scheduler anchors lesson 1 to 1 January 2026 and advances by local civil date, including across New Year and leap day. Tests verify multiple 184-day windows, including boundary-crossing periods, contain 184 distinct lesson IDs.

The guarantee depends on the catalog order and length. Before inserting, deleting, or reordering a lesson, decide whether shifting future daily assignments is acceptable.

## Editorial quality

Automated validation guarantees literal uniqueness, structural validity, stress-word presence, balanced category and level coverage, and date behavior. It cannot judge whether a scenario feels natural. Every seed or dialogue-frame change still needs a human language review and an audio performance review before studio recordings are marked approved.

The catalog is managed in quality-controlled packs: Start Here, Workplace Conversations, Everyday Social English, Difficult Conversations, Travel and Services, and Storytelling and Opinions. Start Here contains 14 individually edited `reviewed` workouts. The other 170 entries remain `draft` roadmap material until each receives the same language review; status is intentionally separate from audio readiness.

The catalog favors useful conversational functions over novelty alone: asking, clarifying, correcting, suggesting, declining, agreeing, supporting, deciding, and closing. Medical-adjacent lessons practice communication only and never provide diagnosis or treatment advice.
