# Six-month curriculum

Well Said contains 184 daily workouts. That number is deliberate: a six-calendar-month span can contain as many as 184 dates, so a 183-lesson catalog is not sufficient for every possible start month.

## Coverage

The rotation balances ten situation families: Everyday, Work, Social, Travel, Food, Home, Services, Community, Wellbeing, and Ideas. Each situation family contributes 18 lessons. The four foundational workouts add a little extra weight to Work, Food, and Social practice. Families are interleaved by lesson number, so the daily rotation rarely repeats a family within two weeks.

Difficulty rotates across A2–B1, B1–B2, and B2–C1, with B1–B2 intentionally receiving the largest share. Ten speaking focuses (content-word stress, thought groups, linking, question intonation, polite rhythm, contrastive stress, turn-taking, clear details, expressive delivery, and calm clarification) each appear in 16–20 lessons and rotate within every situation family.

## Scheduling guarantee

The scheduler anchors lesson 1 to 1 January 2026 and advances by local civil date, including across New Year and leap day. Tests verify multiple 184-day windows, including boundary-crossing periods, contain 184 distinct lesson IDs.

The guarantee depends on the catalog order and length. Before inserting, deleting, or reordering a lesson, decide whether shifting future daily assignments is acceptable.

## Editorial quality

Every workout is a hand-written dialogue with its own arc, specific details, coach notes, expressions, and a purpose-built contrastive-stress exercise. The original template drafts were replaced in a full editorial pass, and an independent review checked naturalness, level fit, stress marking, and safety. All 184 lessons are `reviewed`.

Automated validation guarantees literal uniqueness, structural validity, stress-word presence, balanced category, level, and focus coverage, the absence of template remnants, and date behavior. It cannot judge how a scenario feels to a learner. A pronunciation specialist should still review stress marking and model audio before a lesson is marked `audio-ready`; status is intentionally separate from audio readiness.

New learners begin with Start Here: 14 workouts chosen as a gentle, varied two-week introduction. The other packs are Workplace Conversations, Everyday Social English, Difficult Conversations, Travel and Services, and Storytelling and Opinions.

The catalog favors useful conversational functions over novelty alone: asking, clarifying, correcting, suggesting, declining, agreeing, supporting, deciding, and closing. Medical-adjacent lessons practice communication only and never provide diagnosis or treatment advice.
