# Third-party notices

Well Said locally bundles these fonts through Fontsource:

- Atkinson Hyperlegible Next — SIL Open Font License 1.1
- Newsreader — SIL Open Font License 1.1

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next is the interface and body face, selected for distinguishable letterforms. Newsreader is used selectively for large dialogue text and editorial moments. The Fontsource packages and their included license files are the authoritative copies.

## Model audio

Files in `public/audio/lessons/` are synthetic speech generated with [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) text-to-speech using ElevenLabs premade voices. Per-lesson model and voice details are recorded in `content/audio-provenance.json`. Audio generated on the ElevenLabs free plan is used non-commercially with attribution, which the app shows in the home-page footer. Commercial use requires regenerating the audio on a paid ElevenLabs plan.
