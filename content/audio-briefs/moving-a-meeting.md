# Audio brief — Moving a meeting

Lesson ID: `moving-a-meeting`  
Level: B1–B2  
Target full length: about 46 seconds  
Direction: Collaborative teammates making a small schedule change. Polite and matter-of-fact, with sincere thanks.

## Script and direction

1. Lina: “Are we STILL on / for THREE / this afternoon?” — STILL checks the old plan.
2. Omar: “Actually / could we make it FOUR / instead?” — Brief pause; FOUR corrects the time.
3. Lina: “FOUR works for me. / I’ll UPDATE / the invite.” — Settled falling tone.
4. Omar: “PERFECT. / Thanks / for being FLEXIBLE.” — Slow slightly on the appreciation.
5. Lina: “No WORRIES. / Talk to you THEN.” — Relaxed close; link “talk to you.”

Expressions: “Are we still on?” “Works for me.” “No worries.”

Stress variants for “Could we meet at four instead?”: contrast `we`, `meet`, and `four` clearly.
