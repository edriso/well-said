# Audio brief — Ordering coffee

Lesson ID: `ordering-coffee`  
Level: A2–B1  
Target full length: about 36 seconds  
Direction: A friendly but efficient café exchange. Natural service energy, clear choices, no exaggerated classroom pauses.

## Script and direction

1. Alex: “Hi there! / What can I GET / for YOU?” — Bright greeting; quick “can I.”
2. Sam: “Could I get / a SMALL LATTE / please?” — Smooth “could I”; warm PLEASE.
3. Alex: “Sure. / Would you like that / HOT or ICED?” — Give both choices equal weight.
4. Sam: “ICED, please. / And could I have / OAT milk?” — Pause after please; OAT is new.
5. Alex: “Of COURSE. / That’ll be / FOUR FIFTY.” — Easy contraction; price is clear, not robotic.

Expressions: “Could I get…?” “Of course.”

Stress variants for “Could I get a small latte?”: contrast `I`, `small`, and `latte` naturally.
