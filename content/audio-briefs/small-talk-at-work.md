# Audio brief — Small talk at work

Lesson ID: `small-talk-at-work`  
Level: B1–B2  
Target full length: about 42 seconds  
Direction: Friendly coworkers at the end of an ordinary workday. Relaxed, warm, never overly cheerful. Let the exchange breathe.

## Script and direction

1. Maya: “Hey, how’s your DAY GOING?” — Let “how’s your” flow; curious upward finish.
2. Noah: “Pretty GOOD / I’ve been BUSY / but I’m almost DONE.” — The three strong beats carry the update.
3. Maya: “Nice! Any PLANS / for the WEEKEND?” — Smile; WEEKEND is strongest.
4. Noah: “Yeah / I’m meeting some FRIENDS / on SATURDAY.” — Reduce “some” and “on.”
5. Maya: “Sounds GOOD / I hope you have / a GREAT time.” — Warm and unhurried.
6. Noah: “Thanks! / What about YOU?” — YOU turns the question back.

Expressions: “Pretty good.” “Sounds good.” “What about you?”

Stress variants for “I thought the meeting was tomorrow”: perform separate, credible contrasts on `I`, `meeting`, and `tomorrow`; do not merely raise volume.
