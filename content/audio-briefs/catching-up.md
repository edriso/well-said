# Audio brief — Catching up

Lesson ID: `catching-up`  
Level: B2–C1  
Target full length: about 51 seconds  
Direction: Two friends unexpectedly reconnect. Genuine surprise and affection, with a wider but believable pitch range.

## Script and direction

1. June: “Daniel? / I haven’t seen you / in AGES!” — Name rises in surprise; lengthen AGES slightly.
2. Daniel: “I know! / It’s been WAY too LONG. / How have you BEEN?” — Pause before the real question.
3. June: “Really GOOD, actually. / I just started / a NEW JOB.” — The news lands on NEW JOB.
4. Daniel: “That’s AMAZING. / Tell me / ALL about it.” — Show real enthusiasm.
5. June: “I’d LOVE to. / Do you have TIME / for a COFFEE?” — Light “do you have.”
6. Daniel: “ABSOLUTELY. / Let’s go to that PLACE / around the CORNER.” — Decisive, then relaxed.

Expressions: “In ages.” “Tell me all about it.” “Around the corner.”

Stress variants for “I just started a new job”: contrast `I`, `started`, and `job` naturally.
